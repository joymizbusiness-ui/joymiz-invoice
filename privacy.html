Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Overview

Joymiz Invoice is designed with privacy at its core. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our invoice generator.

Data Collection — None

Joymiz Invoice does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data on our servers. All invoice data — including your business details, client information, line items, and any other content you enter — remains entirely within your web browser.

We have no database. We have no backend. We have no tracking pixels. We do not use cookies for data collection.

How It Works

When you use Joymiz Invoice, all data is processed locally in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is sent to our servers or any third-party service. The application runs entirely on your device.

  • Invoice data — Stays in your browser's memory. Not saved anywhere.
  • Form inputs — Not stored or transmitted. They exist only while the page is open.
  • PDF downloads — Generated locally via your browser's print functionality.

Third-Party Services

Joymiz Invoice does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tools.

Hosting

This website is hosted on Netlify. Netlify's standard infrastructure may collect basic server logs (IP addresses, user agents) for operational purposes. These logs do not contain your invoice data and are managed according to Netlify's own privacy policy.

External Links

Our website may contain links to other Joymiz products or external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Joymiz Invoice is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at our contact page.
